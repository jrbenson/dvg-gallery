# Business Domains

This section contains domain-specific SVG illustrations organized by industry sectors that commonly use data-driven reports and dashboards.

## Available Domains

### [Healthcare](./healthcare/)
Medical and healthcare industry illustrations including medical equipment, patient care symbols, and health monitoring visuals for clinical dashboards and medical reporting systems.

### [Financial Services](./financial-services/)
Banking, insurance, and financial industry illustrations featuring financial instruments, market indicators, and economic symbols for financial dashboards and investment reporting.

### [Manufacturing](./manufacturing/)
Industrial and manufacturing illustrations including machinery, production processes, and quality control symbols for operational dashboards and supply chain analytics.

### [Retail & E-commerce](./retail-ecommerce/)
Retail and online commerce illustrations featuring shopping, inventory, and customer experience symbols for sales dashboards and business intelligence reporting.

### [Technology & SaaS](./technology-saas/)
Technology sector illustrations including software systems, cloud infrastructure, and digital service symbols for performance monitoring and system analytics dashboards.

### [Human Resources](./human-resources/)
Human resources and workforce management illustrations featuring employee-related symbols, organizational charts, and HR process visuals for people analytics dashboards.

### [Marketing](./marketing/)
Marketing and advertising industry illustrations including campaign symbols, customer journey visuals, and brand elements for marketing analytics and performance dashboards.

### [Logistics & Transportation](./logistics-transportation/)
Transportation and logistics illustrations featuring vehicles, shipping, and supply chain symbols for fleet management and delivery tracking dashboards.

### [Energy & Utilities](./energy-utilities/)
Energy sector illustrations including power generation, distribution systems, and utility infrastructure for grid monitoring and energy management dashboards.

### [Education](./education/)
Educational institution illustrations featuring academic symbols, learning processes, and institutional elements for student analytics and educational performance dashboards.
